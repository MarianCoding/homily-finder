So, the person of wisdom sees the world, listen now, not from the narrow perspective of his own needs and desires and preoccupations, not just crawling on that narrow ground right in front of him. Those are the kinds of questions raised by the wise person. Next two gifts of the Spirit are often linked together. Understanding and knowledge. We can move through the day, and we rarely avert to that question.
