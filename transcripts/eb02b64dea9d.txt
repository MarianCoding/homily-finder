At the heart of Catholic spirituality is a great yes to the world and a great no to the world. Getting that right is very tricky. Getting that paradox properly balanced is the key to understanding a lot about Catholic theology and spirituality. Now what do I mean when I say the great yes and the great no? The world, matter, nature, planet Earth, the whole universe is good. The book of Genesis couldn't be clearer. God made all these things and found them good.
